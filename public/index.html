System Architecture

Hierarchical Deep Learning Framework

Instead of building one massive model with unstructured classes, Vanaspati.AI uses a 3-step hierarchical multi-task routing pipeline.

🎯
Step 1: Localization
YOLOv11 Leaf Detection

Detects and crops the leaf, plant, or tree ROI from complex outdoor background noise, soil, and glare.

🌿
Step 2: Species Category
Multi-Task Backbone

EfficientNet-B2 / Vision Transformer classifies species: Spinach & Leafy Greens, Vegetables, Trees, and Crops.

🩺
Step 3: Disease Expert
Specialized Heads

Routes cropped ROI to family-specific expert heads trained to identify subtle pathogen lesions or confirm healthy foliage.
