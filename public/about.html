🌐 Research-Grade Agricultural Artificial Intelligence

Pioneering Autonomous Plant Pathology & Botanical Intelligence

Vanaspati.AI is a next-generation agricultural decision-support ecosystem engineered to identify plant species, diagnose foliar diseases, optimize soil chemistry, and prescribe certified biological and organic treatments. By bridging multi-modal computer vision with domain-specific LLMs (PLLaMa) and Koppert biological standards, we empower farmers, horticulturists, and researchers worldwide.

🔬

Hierarchical Computer Vision Pipeline

Combines YOLOv11 for plant/leaf Region-of-Interest (ROI) localization, a Vision Transformer (ViT) & Multi-Task EfficientNet backbone for species taxonomy across 908 crops, and a specialized ResNet-9 pathology diagnostic head trained on 87,000+ foliar specimens with Focal Loss.

🤖

PLLaMa Plant Science Language Model

Integrates the PLLaMa (arXiv:2401.01600v1) domain-specific LLM framework pretrained on 1,676,389 academic papers across 750 plant science journals. Delivers expert-grounded Q&A for watering regimes, photoperiod lux, N-P-K nutrient balancing, and physiological disorders.

🌿

Certified Biological Protection Standards

Formulates sustainable remedies compliant with Koppert Biological Systems and ICAR Agronomic Standards, utilizing beneficial microbes (Trichoderma harzianum T-22 / Trianum-P, Bacillus subtilis), cold-pressed neem formulations, and bio-rational fungicides.

🧪

Soil Chemistry & Weather Telemetry

Multi-variate Random Forest recommendation engine evaluating active Soil Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, and pH alongside live geographic atmospheric telemetry (Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall via OpenWeather API) across 22 primary commercial crops.

🧬 Unified Multi-Task Inference Flow

How Vanaspati.AI transforms raw leaf photography into actionable agronomic prescriptions:

📸

1. Input Ingestion

User uploads leaf image or captures in field via smartphone camera.

🎯
